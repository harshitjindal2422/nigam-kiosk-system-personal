Project Progress & Workflow Report
Self-Service Citizen Kiosk System — Development Status
Report Date: June 03, 2026 Prepared For: Nagar Nigam Aayukt (IAS) Classification: Official / Internal

1. Executive Summary

The Nagar Nigam Self-Service Kiosk System is a Digital India Initiative designed to bring essential municipal services directly to citizens through easy-to-use, touch-screen kiosk machines placed at public locations. The system eliminates the need for citizens to visit the municipal office for routine services such as downloading birth/death certificates, searching registration records, and submitting correction requests.

The project is currently in active development, with six of the twelve planned phases fully operational and the remaining phases either in progress or scheduled for delivery shortly.

Key Benefit: Every interaction a citizen completes on the kiosk saves an average of one counter visit, reducing waiting time, operational burden, and paper-based record-keeping for the municipal corporation.

2. Project Objective

The kiosk system is designed to provide the following four core services to citizens:

  1. Certificate Download & Print — Citizens can download their birth/death certificates from the Pehchan Portal, pay the applicable fee, and receive a printed copy instantly.
  2. Registration Search — Citizens can search for their registration details through the Pehchan Portal without any staff assistance.
  3. Certificate Correction (Counter Token) — Citizens who need to correct details on a certificate can fill a form, pay the fee, and receive a queue token to be attended to at the counter.
  4. Certificate Correction (Online Portal) — For corrections that can be done digitally, citizens are guided through the Pehchan Portal directly on the kiosk.

3. How the Kiosk Works — Citizen Journey

The following steps describe a typical citizen interaction from start to finish:

# Step What Happens
1 Approach & Wake The citizen taps the screen. A welcome screen appears in Hindi or English (selectable).
2 Select Service Four large service buttons are displayed. The citizen taps the one they need.
3 Enter Details The kiosk asks for basic information (name, mobile number, registration number).
4 Make Payment A UPI QR code is displayed on-screen. The citizen scans and pays from their mobile phone.
5 Receive Output The kiosk prints the certificate or queue token immediately after successful payment.
6 Auto Reset The session is automatically cleared so no personal data remains for the next user.

5. Key Features of the System

  • Bilingual Interface: Citizens can switch between Hindi and English at any time. Voice guidance is also provided.
  • Privacy by Design: All downloaded documents are automatically deleted from the kiosk server immediately after printing. No personal data is retained between sessions.
  • UPI Payment: Citizens pay directly from their mobile phone by scanning a QR code — no cash handling required at the kiosk.
  • Accessibility: Screen magnifier, font-size controls, high-contrast mode, and wheelchair-friendly layout adjustments are built into the kiosk.
  • Auto-Reset: If a citizen walks away without completing the transaction, the kiosk detects inactivity and automatically resets within 60 seconds.
  • Thermal Printing: Certificates and queue tokens are printed on thermal paper — fast, durable, and ink-free.
  • Admin Oversight: Authorised staff can monitor all transactions, payments, and printer status from a dedicated admin panel.
  • Security Hardening: The kiosk browser is restricted to only authorised URLs, preventing misuse by citizens.

7. Conclusion

The Nagar Nigam Kiosk System represents a significant step forward in delivering efficient, transparent, and citizen-friendly municipal services. Six of the twelve planned phases are fully complete, with the remaining phases on a clear and time-bound delivery path.

The development team remains committed to delivering a production-ready, secure, and scalable platform for the Nagar Nigam's citizen services.
